# Breadth Exam

Use this after completing the core curriculum.

## Format

- 6 sections, 2–3 hours each.
- Mix of proofs, design questions, coding/system design, and paper critique.
- Closed-book for fundamentals; open-paper for research critique.

## Sections

1. Algorithms and complexity
2. Math, probability, statistics, and optimization
3. Systems: OS, distributed systems, databases
4. AI/ML foundations
5. Programming languages, compilers, and architecture
6. Research methods and paper critique

## Passing standard

A passing performance demonstrates the ability to:

- Solve graduate-level problems without heavy scaffolding.
- Explain assumptions and tradeoffs.
- Connect fundamentals to research literature.
- Identify limitations in methods and experiments.
