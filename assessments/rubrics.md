# Rubrics

## Paper critique rubric

- Correctly identifies problem and contribution.
- Explains method at an implementable level.
- Evaluates assumptions and limitations.
- Connects to related work.
- Suggests meaningful follow-up work.

## Project rubric

- Clear problem statement.
- Appropriate baselines.
- Reproducible implementation.
- Rigorous evaluation.
- Honest discussion of failure modes.
- Clear technical writing.

## Research maturity rubric

- Can formulate open-ended questions.
- Can narrow broad ideas into testable claims.
- Can design experiments or proofs.
- Can revise based on evidence.
- Can communicate results to technical audiences.
