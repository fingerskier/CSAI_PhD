# Specializations

Choose one primary specialization and one secondary area.

## Available tracks

- [`ai-ml/`](ai-ml/) — AI, machine learning, deep learning, representation learning, RL, generative models.
- [`systems/`](systems/) — Operating systems, distributed systems, databases, infrastructure, ML systems.
- [`theory/`](theory/) — Algorithms, complexity, cryptography, formal methods, theoretical ML.
- [`control-ai/`](control-ai/) — Control systems, robotics, cyber-physical systems, AI-integrated autonomy.

## Specialization expectations

A specialization should include:

- 50–100 paper reading list.
- 2 paper replications.
- 1 survey article.
- 1 original research proposal.
- 1 dissertation-style capstone project.
